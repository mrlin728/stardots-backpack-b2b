# STARDOTS Buyer Enquiries

STARDOTS presents bag sourcing and development services to overseas business buyers. The website helps a buyer choose a reference or describe a project and start a commercial discussion.

## Language

**Sourcing partner**: STARDOTS coordinates bag development, sourcing, production with partner factories, quality planning and delivery. This does not imply ownership of the production facilities.
_Avoid_: Factory owner, own factory

**Reference model**: An existing bag design and its model code used to discuss a project. It is not a promise of stock availability or fixed specifications.
_Avoid_: In-stock item, standard specification

**Enquiry**: A buyer's request to discuss a product or sourcing project. It is not an order or a contractual commitment.
_Avoid_: Purchase, confirmed order

**Qualified enquiry**: An enquiry with enough buyer contact and project context to support a meaningful follow-up. Merely clicking a contact button does not establish an enquiry.
_Avoid_: Click conversion, completed order

**Illustrative visual**: An image used to explain a product direction or process. It does not establish actual manufacturing capacity, certification or an implemented client project.
_Avoid_: Factory proof, customer case

## Confirmed direction

Reference-led bulk sourcing and custom development have equal priority. No additional certifications, client cases, export history, fixed MOQ or lead times have been supplied. The intended primary contact route is the website form delivered by email, with WhatsApp as a fallback. The user selected FormSubmit for email delivery to contact@cnstardots.com. FormSubmit requires recipient activation; provider acceptance alone does not confirm inbox receipt.
